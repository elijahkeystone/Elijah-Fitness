Program structure

A Push/Pull 4-day split hitting every major muscle group twice weekly. Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday are training days. Squats and deadlifts anchor pull sessions and cover your lower body thoroughly.

Philosophy

Modified StrongLifts/HIT hybrid. Compounds first — heavy and progressive. Isolation work follows for aesthetic shape. 60–70 minutes per session. The rule: add weight when you hit the rep ceiling. The workout log flags this automatically.

The goal

Run with your shirt off and feel genuinely comfortable — even attractive. 12 months of consistency → 205–215 lbs with visible shoulder, chest, and back definition. Everything is built backward from that moment.

Rules
Protein first. 200g before anything else.
Weekends count. A few drinks Saturday is fine. Going off-rail Fri–Sun erases 4–5 days of deficit.
Structure over obsession. Same meals, swappable proteins. Don't skip then eat a pizza at 10pm.

Supplements
Layer in tier order — don't jump ahead
Tier 1 Start here
Whey protein isolate
25–30g per scoop · as needed
Fills protein gaps. Supplement meals, don't replace them. Optimum Nutrition, Transparent Labs, or Dymatize.
Creatine monohydrate
5g daily · any time · no cycling
Most researched supplement in existence. Strength, muscle volume, cognitive benefits. Buy cheapest Creapure-certified unflavored you can find.
Magnesium glycinate
300–400mg · 30–45 min before bed
Relaxes nervous system, improves sleep, supports recovery. Non-negotiable given your Tucson sleep environment.
Tier 2 Month 2–3
Vitamin D3 + K2
3,000–5,000 IU D3 · 100mcg K2 · with fattiest meal
Most office workers are deficient. Supports testosterone, immune function, mood.
Fish oil
2–3g EPA/DHA · with food
Reduces training inflammation. Check back label for EPA/DHA numbers. Nordic Naturals or Carlson.
Zinc picolinate
25–30mg · before bed with magnesium
Supports testosterone, immune function, sleep. Don't exceed 40mg without also taking copper.
Tier 3 Once fully consistent
Caffeine + L-theanine
150–200mg caffeine · 200mg L-theanine · pre-workout
Better than most pre-workouts. Black coffee + L-theanine capsule. Clean energy, no crash.
Citrulline malate
6–8g · 30–45 min pre-workout
Genuine pump and improved endurance. Most noticeable once training intensity picks up.
Beta-alanine
3.2g daily · split dose
Delays fatigue in higher rep work. Harmless tingling — split the dose to reduce it.
Skip

Testosterone boosters · fat burners · proprietary pre-workout blends · BCAAs if hitting protein · anything with a label that reads like a chemistry exam.

Sleep — most important variable right now

Inconsistent sleep elevates cortisol, increases hunger 20–30%, kills recovery, suppresses testosterone. Address room temperature first: blackout curtains + window AC. They work better together. Then anchor your wake time, cut screens before bed, take magnesium glycinate.
